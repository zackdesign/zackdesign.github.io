FAQ

Timezone — can you work with a US team?

Yes. Based in Sydney, remote-first since 2016. Strong overlap with US mornings (AU afternoon/evening) and useful async coverage overnight. Standups and review windows are agreed in the SOW.

Expo or bare React Native?

Both. Happy on Expo-managed, Expo bare / prebuild, and fully bare workflows. The audit calls the right shape for your release and native-module constraints — not a religious war.

Who owns the IP?

You do. Work product under the SOW is assigned to the client unless we explicitly agree otherwise (e.g. OSS contributions you want upstreamed). Zack Design does not quietly retain a licence to resell your product code.

Are you available while employed elsewhere?

Zack Design runs as a selective boutique alongside long-running product leadership work. Capacity is finite and scoped on purpose — audits and sprints land when the fit is sharp; this is not a full-time placement desk and not a standing open calendar. If timing doesn't work, you'll get a clear no rather than a half-committed yes.

Do you do staff augmentation?

No. Productised audits, rescue sprints, and fractional lead retainers only. If you need six mid-level tickets closed per week, hire (or use a staff-aug shop). If you need the platform to stop bleeding, talk.

Nitro / performance / offline — is that marketing or the actual wedge?

That's the wedge. Nitro modules, crash/perf triage, and offline-first / maps pipelines are where the CamperMate and OSS work is deepest. Greenfield "make us an app from Figma" is not the offer.
